# Tactics feature research - what we have, and what premium apps have that we do not

Date: 2026-09-26.

## Summary

- **What we have.** The tactics board is complete in three shipped slices ([ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-tactics-scenes-as-versioned-json-in-pitch-metres.md), [ADR 0012](../decisions/0012-animate-tactics-scenes-as-keyframe-steps.md), [ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-tactics-scenes-as-collection-entries.md)): a to-scale FIH pitch in metres with a whole-pitch or short-corner view chosen once at scene creation, home/away tokens with 4-character labels and optional roster links, one ball, three line tools (line, arrow, curved arrow) in 4 colours, 3 widths and solid/dotted, keyframe-step animation with bent runs and per-step lines, playback with speed, undo, save/duplicate/delete, the board over presentation mode, and scenes placed as entries in collections on login-free links. Around it: tagging, clips, collections with notes and insights, presentation mode (pen, laser, board, notes, title cards), still telestration with PNG export, and the clip editor (trim, slow motion, zoom, markers).
- **What is missing.** 54 new features survived de-duplication against the code: 15 small, 17 medium, 9 large, 13 research-grade. The biggest gaps against premium apps are in the drawing vocabulary (semantic run/pass/dribble lines, zones, text), export (image, video, PDF), set-play organisation tied to real clips, and video graphics (spotlight, magnifier, pitch-calibrated drawings). Tracking-based analytics (radar, heatmaps, auto-tracked graphics) are research-grade and depend on the auto-camera roadmap.
- **Recommendation.** Build the top 10 in Part 4, starting with semantic line types, image export and mirror/multi-select, then zones/text and step captions, then the set-play library with plan-vs-reality clip links, video export, and spotlight/magnifier markers.
- **Nothing waits on a decision now.** The ordering in Part 4 is a recommendation to pick from. Where a feature touches a policy (names on login-free links for S4, per-player read receipts against ADR 0009 for M16, audio storage for M7), the row names it and slice 6 gives a safe default (names stay off the link payload), to be settled in that feature's own PR or ADR.

## Part 1 - What the app already has

Read on `develop` at the merge of pull request #201 (2026-09-26). No tactics-related pull requests were open at the time. Paths are repo-relative.

### 1a. Tactics board (`/tactics`, three slices, all shipped; backlog "Later" entry says "The tactics board is complete", [`backlog.md`](backlog.md))

Scene model and storage

- One versioned JSON document per scene in `tactics_scenes.jsonb`, coordinates in pitch metres, `parseScene` the only reader/validator, v1 -> v2 -> v3 upgrade on read (`src/features/tactics/scene.ts`, [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-tactics-scenes-as-versioned-json-in-pitch-metres.md), `drizzle/0009_tactics_scenes.sql`).
- Tokens: player tokens with team `home|away`, a label of max 4 chars (number or short like `TW`), an optional roster `playerId`; at most one ball.
- Limits: 40 tokens, 60 lines, 20 steps, step 0.5-10 s, 100 kB JSON.
- Roster link: token picks a roster player; label becomes shirt number or initials (`src/features/tactics/labels.ts` `rosterLabel`, `src/features/tactics/queries.ts` `listBoardRoster`). The roster name shows only in the accessible name, not on the disc.
- Share links strip roster ids (`scene.ts` `withoutRosterLinks`).

Pitch

- To-scale FIH 2026 pitch drawn from constants: circle, 5 m broken line, penalty spot, 23 m lines, side/back marks, goals, 3 m / 2 m run-off (`src/features/tactics/pitch.ts`, `PitchMarkings.tsx`).
- Views: whole pitch or the short-corner quarter (turned so the goal is on top on landscape), chosen once when the scene is created and fixed after that (`CreateSceneForm.tsx`, `pitch.ts` `PITCH_VIEWS`, `viewBounds`; [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-tactics-scenes-as-versioned-json-in-pitch-metres.md) amendment).
- Phone: pitch turned upright as a view transform (`use-orientation.ts`, `geometry.ts` `Turn`).

Starting arrangements

- New scene starts from a default 1-3-4-3 per side, 11 v 11 numbered 1-11, ball on centre spot (`scene.ts` `defaultScene`), or an empty pitch with only the ball (used in presentation mode). No named formations, no library.
- Add home/away player, add ball; new tokens spawn in the team half or in the middle of the corner quarter.

Drawing

- Three line tools: plain line, straight arrow, curved arrow (quadratic Bezier with a draggable control point) (`scene.ts`, `BoardLineShape.tsx`, `line-paths.ts`).
- 4 pen colours (red, yellow, blue, white), 3 widths, solid or dotted (shared with telestration: `src/features/player/telestration/state.ts`).
- No zones/shapes, no text labels, no freehand, no semantic line types (run vs pass vs dribble are styling conventions only).
- Clear all lines / clear this step's lines.

Editing

- Drag, select, keyboard nudge 0.5 m / 5 m (Shift), Delete, Escape; undo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z, 50-deep history), no redo (`board-state.ts`, `board-keys.ts`).
- Hotkeys: `o` dotted toggle, `w` width cycle, Space play/pause, `b`/`n` step back/forward (`board-keys.ts`).
- Selection panel: label, roster player, remove, per-step run with bend/straighten/reset (`SelectionPanel.tsx`).
- Single selection only; no multi-select, group move, copy/paste, align, mirror/flip.
- Scene list, create (name and view), rename, save (explicit Save button with dirty state), duplicate, delete with confirm (`actions.ts`, `ScenesList.tsx`, `SceneEditor.tsx`).

Animation ([ADR 0012](../decisions/0012-animate-tactics-scenes-as-keyframe-steps.md))

- Keyframe steps: step 0 = start; each step lists only the tokens that move, straight or bent through a `via` point; one duration per step; ease-in-out; lines belong to a step (`scene.ts`, `animation.ts`).
- Pure engine `frameAt(scene, t)` shared by editor, presentation and share link.
- Transport: play/pause, restart, step back/forward, scrubber, time readout, speed ladder shared with the video player (`StepsBar.tsx`, `SpeedButton`, `board-state.ts`).
- Not supported ([ADR 0012](../decisions/0012-animate-tactics-scenes-as-keyframe-steps.md) "Revisit"): different speeds within one step, holds between steps, ball automatically following a player, overlapping runs.

Presentation and sharing of scenes

- Board over presentation mode with `t`: default lineup, empty pitch or a saved scene (coach-only load via `GET /api/tactics/scenes/[id]`); never saved (`PresentationBoard.tsx`, `src/app/api/tactics/scenes/[id]/route.ts`).
- Scenes as collection entries placed after a clip, hold time for still scenes (3-60 s), live by reference, played on the login-free collection link and in presentation mode ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-tactics-scenes-as-collection-entries.md), `drizzle/0011_collection_scenes.sql`, `src/features/share/collections/scene-entries.ts`, `SceneEntriesEditor.tsx`).
- A scene has no share link of its own and no export (no PNG/GIF/MP4/PDF).

### 1b. Video features a tactics feature can build on

- Tagging: 4 tag types (Tor, Ecke kurz, Aktion gut, Aktion schlecht) with hotkeys and default clip windows (`contracts/tag-types.json`), linked to players, on global game time ([ADR 0002](../decisions/0002-global-game-time-offset-model.md)).
- Clips: tag -> ffmpeg cut job -> playable clip ([ADR 0004](../decisions/0004-copy-cut-clips-with-keyframe-tolerance.md), [ADR 0007](../decisions/0007-clip-worker-lives-in-the-app-repo.md)); visibility team vs single player.
- Share links: login-free team, per-player and collection links, noindex, per-player `single` isolation (`src/features/share/team`, `share/player`, `share/collections`).
- Collections with clip + scene entries, team-visible texts, private presenter notes, view insights (clicks, full views, replays, viewers per day; [ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-anonymous-view-counting-on-collection-links.md)) (`share/collections/*`, `share/views/*`, `CollectionInsights.tsx`).
- Presentation mode: fullscreen playback, pen (`d`), laser pointer (`p`), board (`t`), notes (`h`), markers toggle (`m`), title cards, text size scale (`src/features/share/presentation/*`).
- Telestration on a paused frame: arrow, curved arrow, circle, freehand; 4 colours, 3 widths, dotted; PNG still export at native resolution (`src/features/player/telestration/state.ts`, `export.ts`).
- Clip editor ([ADR 0011](../decisions/0011-clip-edits-are-data-rendered-at-playback.md)): trim, slow motion, zoom keyframes (hold/glide), markers drawn with the telestration tools that freeze or run on, shown/hidden by viewers (`src/features/clip-editor/{trim,slow,zoom,marks}.ts`). MP4 export with edits burned in is a later optional slice (`backlog.md` "Later").
- Comments per clip on team/player links with coach-highlighted comments ([`coach-guide.md`](coach-guide.md) section 5).
- Reports: per-game and team figures by quarter and player, CSV export (`src/features/reports`).
- Mac app plan: the native Mac app reuses the scene format unchanged; S9 Scenes API (bearer auth, JSON schema contract), M16 tactics board on the Mac, M17 animation and scenes in presentations ([`mac-app-plan.md`](mac-app-plan.md)).
- Auto-camera roadmap: panorama + virtual camera via `reco-project/video-stitcher` (tracking is used for framing only); player statistics explicitly out of scope for year one ([`roadmap-auto-camera.md`](roadmap-auto-camera.md)). A ball model is trained in S12-S13.

## Part 3 - The new list: features the app does not have yet

Scoring: **Value** is for a field-hockey coach of one club (the app's user today): high = used every week in session prep or team talks, medium = regularly useful, low = nice to have. **Difficulty** is for this stack (Next.js, SVG board in pitch metres, versioned scene JSON with `parseScene`, pure animation engine, ffmpeg worker, Mac app, no realtime server, no ML in the app): small = one PR under ~1k lines, medium = one or two PRs under ~2k lines each, large = several PRs or a new subsystem, research-grade = needs computer vision / ML or hardware we do not have. Source keys like [TP] are listed in Part 5; Appendix A has the full survey with one URL per claim.

## Part 4 - Recommended order: top 10 as PR-sized slices

Assumes Q2 and Q3 land first. Each slice ships on its own, keeps the scene format upgrade-on-read (one new `version` per slice that touches the document, per [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-tactics-scenes-as-versioned-json-in-pitch-metres.md)), and stays under ~2k lines including tests and docs. Every scene-format bump also has to reach the Mac contract (`contracts/schemas/tactics-scene.schema.json`, planned in S9 of [`mac-app-plan.md`](mac-app-plan.md)), since the Mac reuses the web format unchanged. Ordered by value per effort, with dependencies first.

1. **Semantic line types (S1)** - scene v4 with run / pass / dribble / block tools, a legend, hotkeys; old lines map to their current look. About 0.8k. Unblocks M1 and M9 legends.
2. **Board image export and share (S7)** - PNG of the current step with aspect presets, Web Share API on phones. About 0.6k. The quickest answer to "export for team chats".
3. **Mirror/flip plus editing ergonomics (S6 + S8)** - flip left-right/top-bottom, marquee multi-select, group move, copy/paste, redo. About 1.4k. Makes Q3 formations and corner variants cheap to author.
4. **Zones and text callouts (S2 + S3)** - one new element kind (shape or text) with step membership and corner-view clipping. About 1.6k.
5. **Step captions, holds and scene notes (S9)** - captions shown on the link and in presentation, holds after a step, private coaching points. About 1.0k.
6. **Player names on tokens (S4)** - name tag toggle, position codes; needs the privacy call on names reaching login-free links (default suggestion: names only on the coach's board and in presentation mode for the signed-in coach, never in the link payload, matching `withoutRosterLinks`). About 0.7k.
7. **Set-play library, part 1 (S12 + M3 grouping)** - categories and tags on scenes (attack corner, defence corner, free hit, press), filter and search on `/tactics`. About 1.2k.
8. **Set-play library, part 2: plan vs reality (M3)** - link a scene to tagged "Ecke kurz" clips, show the executions and a success count next to the scene, open them as a playlist. About 1.8k.
9. **Animated scene to video (M1)** - client-side MP4 (WebCodecs, GIF fallback) from `frameAt`, captions burned in. About 1.5k.
10. **Spotlight ring and magnifier markers on clips (M11)** - two new telestration/marker tools in the clip editor, played on links and in presentation. About 1.5k. Sets up M12 (keyframed moving drawings) as the next slice.

Next in line after the top 10: attached lines and areas (M6), player quiz on the link (M2), ball possession and pass flight (M5), second-screen presenter view (M8), and manual pitch calibration on video (L2) as the first large bet.

### Field hockey specific observations

- Tools that explicitly support field hockey: Sportplan (drill library 1,000+ drills, action-based Animator), planet.training (field hockey drawing tool, 12 perspectives, 30-scene animation), Field Hockey Tactic Board (Naoya Ono; player photos, variable player count), Coach's Tactic Board App and Coach Tactic Board Hockey++ (field hockey among supported sports), Once Coach Board, VidSwap, Hudl Sportscode (penalty-corner databases, mirror coding, GPS), Nacsport (free field hockey tagging windows and dashboards).
- Hockey Coach Vision is ice hockey, inline hockey and ringette only (App Store listing); it is still a useful reference for 2D/3D animated drills, audio commentary and a player app.
- Hudl Sportscode's field hockey article highlights penalty corners (roughly 40 percent of goals) as the key set piece to build databases around - relevant for linking set-play boards to tagged "Ecke kurz" clips.

### Ideas most directly relevant to linking tactics and video (synthesis)

These are observations from the survey, not features of a single app:

1. Board-from-frame: DrawTactics converts a match still into a board via CV; a manual variant (pause clip, place tokens over the frame, save as board step) is feasible without CV.
2. Board-to-video overlay: Coach Paint / Chyron "virtual formation" and Hudl/Once "move player" show the value of rendering a board state over a calibrated video frame (needs a pitch homography; manual 4-point calibration is a simpler path than AI calibration).
3. Stats-to-clip linking (Sportscode Matrix, Hudl Assist, Nacsport dashboards) and set-piece databases pair naturally with a set-play library: each set play can list its tagged real executions.
4. Freeze-frame + animated drawings (KlipDraw Basic/Animate) is the lowest-cost telestration pattern that fits an SVG keyframe engine already in place.
